# Public Faculty-Facing Resources and Entry Points

Scope: the public pages a faculty member is most likely to need — Provost's Office,
the faculty and staff hub, directories, faculty governance notices, Registrar,
library and educational technology, IT, the Accessible Learning Center, teaching and
writing support, research administration, and the Regulations Manual. This file maps
entry points and says what each one actually gives you. Deadlines and system
mechanics are in `resources/faculty/deadlines-and-systems.md`; office ownership for
non-academic questions is in `resources/shared/office-routing.md`.

**Verified through: 2026-08-31**

**Audience:** faculty. **Volatility:** the URLs and office descriptions below are
stable; the named individuals and phone numbers are annual-volatility facts and are
kept to the minimum needed for routing.

---

## The two faculty hubs, and what they are not

**`/faculty-staff/` — Resources for Faculty and Staff.** A thin routing page, not a
knowledge base. Retrieved 2026-08-31 it offers exactly eight links, grouped as campus
directories (Faculty and Staff Directory, Department Directory, AOC Directory),
teaching resources (Academic Calendar, Chart Your Course Curriculum, Course
Descriptions and Schedule via the Registrar), and campus resources — where the single
entry is **"NCF Intranet (MyNCF Login Required)."** A faculty member who lands here
expecting substance will be sent to the intranet.

**`/departments/provost/` — Office of the Provost.** The substantive public faculty
hub. Its Academic and Faculty Resources menu is the best public index of faculty
material, and it mixes public documents with authenticated ones. Sorted by what you
actually get:

| Menu item | Where it goes | Public? |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Reference Guide | Provost's Office PDF, faculty administrative and academic processes | **Yes** |
| Academic Contract | Undergraduate catalog, academic contract and ISP | **Yes** |
| Student Assessment Policy | NCF Policy 4.1001 PDF | **Yes** |
| Academic Honor Code and Guidelines | NCF page | **Yes** |
| Summary of General Education / General Education 2026 | PDFs | **Yes** |
| Faculty Travel Guidelines | Travel expense information PDF | **Yes** |
| Student Field Trip Waiver | Template PDF | **Yes** |
| Faculty Committees | Faculty committee notices and minutes page | **Yes** |
| New Faculty Resources | `/departments/resources-for-new-faculty/` | **Yes** |
| Grade Equivalency Request (former students) | Dynamic Forms, non-SSO | **Yes** |
| Grade Equivalency Request (current) | Dynamic Forms via myNCF SSO | No |
| Academic Forms | `intranet.ncf.edu/registrar-forms/` | No |
| Faculty Resources | Intranet Provost's resources-for-faculty page | No |
| Faculty Travel Info. | Intranet procurement purchasing information | No |
| Civic Literacy Study Guide | Library LibGuides link | Reachable, not verified in this corpus |

The Provost's Office also lists office contact — phone (941) 487-4203, `provost@ncf.edu`,
College Hall, Monday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. — and publishes its team with
titles. As of 2026-08-31 the page lists a Provost and Vice President of Academic
Affairs, an Associate Provost for Academic and Faculty Affairs, an Associate Provost
for Student Academic Affairs, a Director of Teaching and Learning, and a Director of
Academic Operations and Special Projects. Prefer the office alias over a personal
address, and re-verify a name before repeating it: the page currently shows at least
one staff card whose "View Full Profile" link resolves to a different person's
directory entry, so the roster is not internally consistent.

## Resources for New Faculty

`/departments/resources-for-new-faculty/` is short and specific, and worth knowing
because it answers three real onboarding questions:

- **Canvas access.** The page states that a guest account is created for each new
  faculty member and instructions are emailed to the address on the CV, and that once
  an NCF email address exists the guest account can be transferred to it. Canvas itself
  is not public.
- **Classroom lab software requests.** Requests go to IT by email with the subject
  "Lab Software Request," and the page states IT needs the version or build number, a
  copy of or link to the media, and licensing information. It describes lab updates as
  happening at the end of each semester for the next term.
- **Relocation program.** A named real-estate relocation benefit for incoming faculty
  and staff, with rebate and closing-cost terms stated on the page. This is a benefit
  description with dollar figures and should be re-verified before being repeated; it
  carries no effective date.

## Directories

- **Faculty and Staff Directory** — `https://www.ncf.edu/directory/`. The general
  people search, and the right first answer to "who do I contact."
- **Faculty Biography Index** — `https://www.ncf.edu/faculty-index/`. Faculty by name
  with rank, department, and a research description. Useful for identifying a
  colleague's field or division; not a source for whether someone currently holds an
  administrative role.
- **Department Directory** — `https://www.ncf.edu/departments/`. Offices and
  departments, the backbone of `resources/shared/office-routing.md`.
- **AOC Directory** — `https://www.ncf.edu/programs/`. Areas of concentration and
  programs.

## Faculty governance: what is genuinely public

`https://www.ncf.edu/faculty-committee-agendas-and-minutes/` — "Faculty Committees,
Public Notices and Agendas." Retrieved 2026-08-31 it publishes Academic Affairs
Committee (AAC) public notices, agendas, and minutes as dated PDFs organized by fiscal
year, alongside a Standing Committees section and links for Fall 2025-Spring 2026,
Fall 2024-Spring 2025, and Fall 2023-Spring 2024.

What this supports: telling a faculty member that AAC notices, agendas, and minutes
are posted publicly and roughly how far back the archive runs, and pointing at a
specific dated PDF.

What it does not support: describing the membership, charge, or current business of
any committee from memory. The August 2023 Faculty Handbook describes the committee
structure — Educational Policy Committee, Faculty Appointments and Status Committee,
Student Academic Status Committee, Provost's Advisory Committee, Institutional Review
Board, and others — but that is a 2023 description of structure, not a current roster.
Confidential committee deliberations are out of scope entirely; see
`resources/faculty/public-coverage-gaps.md`.

## Registrar

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/registrar/` — (941) 487-4230, `registrar@ncf.edu`,
Palmer D, Monday to Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Sub-pages that matter to faculty:
Academic Calendars and Catalogs, Class Schedule and Registration, Transcripts and
Records, and Veterans and Military-Affiliated Student Services.

The Registrar is the confirming office for essentially every academic-record question
a faculty member cannot answer from public documents: which deadline binds for a named
term, whether a specific student's standing has changed, whether an override or late
form was accepted, and which evaluation rule applies to a given cohort.

Course offerings and section-level data are Agent 6's territory — use those tools
rather than reading a schedule page by hand.

## Library, educational technology, and the Academic Resource Center

- **Jane Bancroft Cook Library** — `https://www.ncf.edu/departments/jane-bancroft-cook-library/`,
  (941) 487-4411, `library@ncf.edu`, Library 100. Public sub-pages cover research
  support, services, and research librarians.
- **Educational Technology Services (ETS)** — `https://www.ncf.edu/departments/educational-technology-services/`,
  (941) 487-4306, `edtech@ncf.edu`, Library 120, Monday to Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
  ETS is a unit within the library and is the public entry point for Canvas
  administration and assistance, AV equipment checkout for classroom and educational
  project use, AV support for ISP, tutorial, thesis, baccalaureate exam, and guest
  lecture events, instructional technology workshops and support, Google Workspace and
  Microsoft 365, Adobe Creative Suite, the Media Lab, and large-format academic poster
  printing. Its page also routes several things away from itself: laptop repair to
  outside shops, AV for non-academic events to Campus Space Scheduling, marketing
  posters to the USF Print Center, and classroom furniture or whiteboard issues to
  Physical Plant.
- **Academic Resource Center (ARC)** — `https://www.ncf.edu/departments/academic-resource-center/`,
  941-487-4742, Library 119. A physical tutoring location; the page states that
  scheduling and the tutoring partner run through tiles on the myNCF portal, so the
  booking workflow itself is not public.

## Accessible Learning Center

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/advocacy-accessibility/` — (941) 487-4844,
`aalc@ncf.edu`, Hamilton Classroom 005. The faculty-specific page is
`/departments/advocacy-accessibility/faculty-resources/` and the testing page is
`/departments/advocacy-accessibility/testing-and-classroom-accommodations/`
(`testingcenter@ncf.edu`, same phone; testing by appointment Monday to Friday 9:00
a.m. to 4:30 p.m., earliest exam start 9:00 a.m., latest 3:00 p.m., HCL 5).

The faculty page carries real operating rules — refer accommodation requests to the
ALC, never deny an accommodation without consulting ALC staff first, respect
non-disclosure — plus digital accessibility guidance for PDFs and media. Its two
deeper items, "Information for Faculty" and "FAQs for Faculty," both link to the
intranet and are not public. The accommodation rules themselves are summarized in
`resources/faculty/academic-workflows.md`.

## Teaching and writing support

- **Writing Program, Faculty Writing Resources** —
  `https://www.ncf.edu/academics/writing-program/faculty-writing-resources/`. Public
  offer: consultation with the Director or Assistant Director of Writing on writing
  outcomes, integrating formal and informal writing, scaffolding assignments, and
  faculty workshops; and partnership with the Writing Resource Center for one-on-one or
  recurring student writing conferences, in-class or out-of-class workshops,
  WRC-facilitated peer review, and classroom writing materials. Contact runs through
  `/departments/writing-program/`. Deeper faculty material — writing-enhanced courses,
  faculty writing plans, thesis support for faculty, faculty writing FAQs — is on the
  intranet community pages and is not public.
- **Career Engagement and Opportunity (CEO), faculty and staff page** —
  `https://www.ncf.edu/ceo/faculty-staff/`. Public: a request form to have CEO teach a
  career workshop in a class or assign a career activity, guidance on assigning a career
  activity as a contract goal, an on-campus student employment posting form, and
  internship resources for faculty creating an internship. LinkedIn Learning is
  described but is reached through myNCF.
- **Director of Teaching and Learning** — listed on the Provost's Office team page with
  a Quality Matters and teaching-online certificate role. Route teaching-development
  questions to the Provost's Office rather than guessing at a program.

## Research administration

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/research-programs-services/` — Office of Research
Programs and Services, `orps@ncf.edu`, with a separate `IRB@ncf.edu` address. This is
the public entry point for sponsored programs, active awards, and the Institutional
Review Board. The public "Report a Problem" page routes research non-compliance —
ethical standards, federal regulations, other laws, institutional policies — to the
IRB. Do not describe IRB submission mechanics; they are not public in this corpus.

## Regulations Manual: the public authority for employment and governance topics

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/office-of-the-general-counsel/regulations-policies-procedures/`
is the most under-used public faculty source. NCF promulgates regulations under the
Florida Board of Governors regulation development process, and the page states that the
Regulations Manual supersedes and replaces previously adopted rules and policies. The
regulations are posted as individual public PDFs.

Regulations that answer faculty questions people assume are private:

| Regulation | Topic |
|---|---|
| 3-4009 | Grievances — applies to General Faculty and A&P employees, subject to any applicable collective bargaining agreement |
| 3-4018 | Sexual Discrimination / Harassment — the NCF Title IX Compliance Policy, effective June 26, 2025 |
| 3-4027 | Discrimination / Harassment |
| 3-4007 | Misconduct |
| 3-4015 | Limited-Access Personnel Records |
| 4-8001 | Faculty Post-Tenure Review |
| 1-1009 | Student Records |
| 1-1016 | ADA |
| 6-3005 | Student Code of Conduct |
| 4-6002 / 4-6304 / 4-6308 | Research misconduct, fiscal misconduct in sponsored research, responsible conduct of research training |

So the correct answer to "is there anything public about post-tenure review or
grievances?" is yes — the regulation is public — while the *internal procedure, a
committee's deliberations, and any individual case* remain out of scope. Distinguish
those two things every time.

The same office publishes a general contact route: Office of the General Counsel,
941-487-4877, `generalcounsel@ncf.edu`.

## Human Resources: public surface only

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/human-resources/` — (941) 487-5020, `hr@ncf.edu`,
Palmer D. The public page covers the HR mission, employment and open positions, and
employee benefits. It does not publish individual compensation, personnel review
procedure, or leave adjudication. Route salary, benefits enrollment, leave, and
employment-status questions to HR directly and say plainly that the answer is not in
public sources. Public regulations 3-4001, 3-4008, 3-4009, and 3-4015 exist for
classification, appointment, grievances, and personnel records and can be cited as the
published framework.

## Compliance and audit

`https://www.ncf.edu/departments/internal-audit-compliance-office/` — the Chief Audit
Executive and Chief Compliance Officer, named on the public "Report a Problem" page as
the route for compliance, ethics, or misconduct issues. Waste, fraud, or financial
mismanagement reporting is described on that page, including a downloadable complaint
form and external routes. See `resources/shared/sensitive-referrals.md`.

# Faculty-Side Public Deadlines and Systems

Scope: when faculty-side academic work is due, which system each step runs in, and
which of those systems can be reached publicly. Deadlines here are stated the way the
public sources state them — as relative weeks of the term or as rules — because the
absolute dates for a named term belong to the official academic calendar. This file
never asserts a calendar date.

**Verified through: 2026-08-31**

**Audience:** faculty. **Applicability:** undergraduate contract system; the
week-numbered timeline below is labeled by its source as the Fall 2025 / Spring 2026
faculty guide.

> **Get absolute dates from the calendar, not from here.** For any specific term, use
> the official NCF academic calendar and `shared-academic-calendar`. Two public NCF
> sources disagree about
> several of the deadlines below; both readings are recorded rather than merged.

---

## Systems map and public reachability

Verified 2026-08-31 by anonymous request, with no credentials and no saved session.

| System | What faculty do there | Public? |
|---|---|---|
| myNCF portal (`myncf.ncf.edu`) | Front door for every other faculty system | **No** — returns a login page |
| NCF Intranet (`intranet.ncf.edu`) | Provost faculty resources, registrar forms, ALC faculty fast facts, community pages | **No** — every path tested returns the same myNCF login portal |
| SES (`ses.ncf.edu`) | Narrative evaluations; contract criteria for advisees | **No** — redirects to myNCF single sign-on |
| Banner Faculty Self Service | Course designations, contract certification, incompletes, registration overrides, tutorial creation, senior thesis evaluation, baccalaureate exam report | **No** — reached through a myNCF tile |
| Navigate 360 | Ad hoc alerts, mid-semester progress reports, office hours, advisee unofficial transcript | **No** — not publicly reachable |
| Dynamic Forms via NextGen SSO | Student contract, tutorial proposal, course add/drop, written student complaint | **No** — SSO required |
| Dynamic Forms without SSO | Grade Equivalency Request for **former** students | **Yes** — the Provost's Office links a non-SSO version of this one form |
| Canvas, RegisterBlast, AIM | Course launch and exam submission for ALC testing; Flex Plans | **No** |
| Maxient (`cm.maxient.com`) | **Title IX Incident Report** and **Student Support Team: Student of Concern** | **Yes** — both forms load without login |

Practical consequence: a faculty member can be told *what* to do and *where the door
is* for any of these, and can be walked all the way through the two Maxient reports,
but the inside of the authenticated systems is not public and must not be described
from memory. See `resources/faculty/public-coverage-gaps.md`.

## Term timeline as published by the Provost's Office

From the Quick Reference Guide, "Registration and Financial Aid Timeline," labeled
Fall 2025 / Spring 2026. Weeks are counted from the start of the semester.

> **Scope limit.** Only the faculty-facing markers are summarized here. The same Guide
> section also publishes refund percentages, housing and meal plan refund points, and
> payment-plan deadlines. Those are tuition, billing, and calendar facts owned by other
> resources — `shared-academic-calendar` for dated Registrar deadlines and
> `outside-cost-and-aid` for refund and billing amounts. Cite those
> rather than repeating an amount from this file, and route a student's money question
> to Student Accounts or Financial Aid via `resources/shared/office-routing.md`.

**Week 1 — add/drop.** Students add or drop online through student self-service.
**End of Week 1: registration and contract submission deadline.** Students who have not
completed the required three ISPs may have ISP block courses added so tuition and fees
bill correctly; a student not intending to complete an ISP must drop that block course
in this window to avoid being charged.

**Week 2 — reinstatement and late registration.** Adds and drops move to the Course
Add/Drop Form, and **advisor approval is required to add a course** — the first point in
the term where a faculty signature gates a student's registration. A new registration
this week may incur a late registration fee. End of Week 2 is also **the last day to add
a full-term course without a Provost petition**.

Two consequences an advisor should raise before that boundary passes, both stated in the
Guide: **a course dropped after Week 2 counts as attempted for Satisfactory Academic
Progress**, and with the exception of Bright Futures most financial aid will not be
adjusted for changes made after this point, including Module II courses. The advisor's
role here is to flag the boundary, not to work out the student's aid; see
`resources/faculty/public-advising-responsibilities.md`.

**Weeks 5-13.** Module II courses may be added with the Course Add/Drop Form until the
Module II add deadline, with payment required immediately on registration and aid
possibly unavailable for those additions. The Guide gives three approximate markers: the
**Module II add deadline is usually week 9**; the **ISP documentation deadline — the last
day to submit ISP Course Description forms — is usually week 13**; and the **withdrawal
and course drop deadline is late in the term, usually week 13**, after which a dropped
course's hours still count as attempted.

**Mid-semester progress reports** are requested at **Weeks 5 and 10** through Navigate
360 campaigns or an emailed link.

**Syllabi** must be available to students — print or online — in the first week, with
copies generally filed with the Division Chair or the Provost's Office.

> **Source defect worth knowing.** In the published Guide, the Weeks 5-13 block is
> headed "Deadlines (End of Week 4)" while listing week 9 and week 13 items. Read the
> week numbers in the body, and confirm anything that matters against the academic
> calendar.

## Evaluation and certification deadlines: two public sources disagree

**Narrative evaluations.**

- *Quick Reference Guide (Provost's Office, updated February 2026):* narrative
  evaluations are **generally due by Wednesday of the 16th week**, entered in SES.
- *Faculty Handbook (August 2023):* evaluations submitted after **5:00 p.m. on
  Wednesday of the eighteenth week** after the beginning of the semester are
  considered late.

Do not average these and do not pick one silently. The Guide is newer and is the
Provost's Office's current operational statement; the Handbook is the older governance
document. Tell the faculty member both readings exist and route the binding date to
the Office of the Registrar and the term's academic calendar.

Both sources agree on two related points: the Registrar sets **earlier** deadlines for
students under SASC probationary review and for prospective graduates, and evaluations
must be submitted for every student on the final course registration list issued after
the renegotiation deadline. The Handbook adds that an unsatisfactory evaluation is not
required for a student who has officially withdrawn, and that an evaluation of work for
contract entry is due two weeks from the date an "All Work Submitted" form is filed.

**Contract submission.**

- *Guide:* registration and contract submission deadline at the **end of Week 1**, with
  Week 2 as a reinstatement and late-registration period.
- *Handbook:* the eContract is due in the Registrar's Office by **5:00 p.m. on the
  second Wednesday of the term**, with late submission and a fee accepted until close
  of business on the **second Friday**; a student whose contract is not in by then has
  enrollment canceled unless they successfully petition the Provost.

These are close but not the same, and the consequence stated in the Handbook —
cancellation of enrollment — is serious enough that it should be confirmed with the
Registrar rather than paraphrased as settled.

**Contract renegotiation.** The Handbook states renegotiation is open until the
beginning of the **twelfth week**, with both signatures required, and that after that a
petition to the Provost's Office is needed and is granted only in exceptional
circumstances.

**Contract certification.** The Handbook states certifications are due in the Office of
the Registrar **10 days after the first day of the following term**. The Guide places
the action in Banner under Enter Grades and does not restate the deadline.

**Incomplete work.** The Handbook states that course and tutorial undertakings still
incomplete **one calendar year** from the first day of the semester the contract was
written for automatically become unsatisfactory. Separately, an incomplete *contract
certification* must stipulate a completion deadline, which supersedes deadlines
instructors set on individual undertakings. The Guide notes that selecting "Incomplete"
in Faculty Self Service auto-populates a default deadline date.

## ISP deadlines

From the Faculty Handbook:

- The ISP Description form, approved by both the project advisor and the contract
  sponsor, is due to the Registrar's Office by **5 p.m. on December 1**, or the next
  business day if December 1 falls on a weekend. Late proposals are assessed a fee.
- The **renegotiation deadline** is 5:00 p.m. on the third business day of the ISP
  period. (The Handbook states this as "third business day" in one place and "third
  day" in another; treat the exact day as unconfirmed.) Renegotiating an ISP that was
  submitted on time is not assessed a late fee.
- A student registered for an ISP who does not submit an Interterm ISP description form
  by the renegotiation deadline **forfeits payment and the ISP is recorded
  Unsatisfactory**.
- A January ISP not evaluated Satisfactory in the Student Evaluation System by 5:00
  p.m. on the Wednesday of the eighteenth week after the beginning of the subsequent
  Spring semester is designated Unsatisfactory; a summer ISP is treated the same way
  relative to the subsequent Fall semester, with an expected completion date at the
  beginning of Fall.
- Changing an ISP's topic and advisor after the renegotiation deadline requires a
  successful petition to the Provost.

The Guide's "ISP documentation deadline … usually week 13" appears to describe the same
ISP Course Description filing inside the term-week framing. The December 1 date and a
week-13 date will not coincide in every calendar, so confirm the term's actual deadline
against the academic calendar.

## Delinquent evaluations and certifications: the stated consequence

The Faculty Handbook states this plainly, and a faculty member asking about a missed
deadline should hear it:

- The Registrar's Office notifies the **Provost** and the appropriate **Division
  Chairperson**, who forwards notice to each delinquent faculty member.
- The notification is **included in the faculty member's permanent file for review
  during retention, promotion, tenure, and salary considerations**.
- The Provost may send written reminders as soon as possible after the due date, and
  two weeks after the due date the Registrar may prepare a list of still-delinquent
  faculty that the Provost sends to all faculty and academic administrators; those lists
  are kept on file with the Registrar and made available to the Provost's Advisory
  Committee on request.

This is a 2023 Handbook statement. Present it as the published consequence, and note
that the current practice should be confirmed with the Provost's Office. Whether a
specific faculty member is on such a list is a personnel matter and is out of scope.

## Registration overrides

The Provost's Office publishes a one-page override instruction sheet, and the Quick
Reference Guide repeats the steps. The workflow: log in to myNCF, open the Faculty Self
Service tile, choose **Registration Overrides** under Faculty Tools, select the term,
enter the student's ID including the leading **N**, choose the override type —
**Course Capacity** for a waitlist override, **Course Prerequisite** for a prerequisite
override — select the course, add another override if needed, and submit.

Two points the Guide emphasizes: a student ID is available through the student profile
or SES, and **granting an override does not register the student** — the faculty member
must tell the student to go register.

> **Freshness caution.** The override sheet is published under a filename naming Spring
> 2026 but its own footer reads "Updated 02/08/2024." Treat the screen names as
> possibly dated and confirm with the Registrar if the menus do not match.

## Faculty-side reporting actions and where they run

| Action | System | Public? |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative evaluation | SES → My Course Schedule → term → course | No |
| Course designation (Sat+/Sat/Sat-/Unsat/Incomplete) | Banner Faculty Self Service → Enter Grades | No |
| Contract review and signature | Faculty Self Service → Review and Sign Contracts, or the emailed per-student link | No |
| Contract certification | Banner → Enter Grades → certification status | No |
| Course assessment / grade change form | Faculty Self Service | No |
| Registration override | Faculty Self Service → Registration Overrides | No |
| Tutorial creation | Faculty Self Service | No |
| Senior thesis or project evaluation | Faculty Self Service | No |
| Baccalaureate Exam Report (BER) | Faculty Self Service, after a successful defense | No |
| Flag a student of concern (ad hoc alert) | Navigate 360 → student profile → Issue an Alert | No |
| Mid-semester progress report | Navigate 360 campaign or emailed link, Weeks 5 and 10 | No |
| Report an Honor Code violation | Maxient tile in the NCF Portal | No |
| **Report a Title IX concern** | Maxient Title IX Incident Report | **Yes** |
| **Refer a student to the Student Support Team** | Maxient Student of Concern form | **Yes** |
| ALC exam scheduling | Canvas → ALC Testing Center → RegisterBlast | No |
| Flex Plan | AIM | No |

The two public rows matter: they are the faculty referral actions the bot can support
end to end. See `resources/shared/sensitive-referrals.md`.

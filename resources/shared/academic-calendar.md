# Academic Calendar (Public Dates)

Scope: Current public academic-calendar dates for New College of Florida, as published by the College on its public Academic Calendars page. This resource carries dates only. Rules that govern what a date means (contract certification, ISP requirements, AOC milestones, refunds) live in the topic resources under `resources/students/`.

Verified through: 2026-08-31

Applies to: academic year 2026-2027. Separate undergraduate and graduate calendars; they do not agree on every date. The published catalog edition at verification was 2025-2026.

Volatility: `term`. Re-verify at the start of every term, and before quoting any date to a user. Dates below are transcribed from a public page and are not a substitute for the live calendar.

---

## How to read this resource

- The undergraduate and graduate calendars are **separate published calendars** and they do not agree on every date. Do not merge them. Determine the student's program level first.
- Every date below is transcribed as published, including apparent inconsistencies. Known discrepancies are listed in "Apparent discrepancies" and are **not** silently corrected here.
- Deadline times matter. Where the source gives a time (4:00 pm, 5:00 pm), quote it.
- This calendar does not establish course availability, seats, or whether a specific section meets. Course-level questions go to the course-data resources owned by Agent 6.

## Apparent discrepancies and confirmation needs

These are transcribed as published. **Do not repair them in an answer.** Tell the user what the sources say, that they disagree, and that the Office of the Registrar confirms.

1. **Catalog edition lags the calendar year.** At the verification date the published catalog edition was labeled **2025-2026**, while the public academic calendar showed **2026-2027**. Cite the calendar for 2026-2027 dates and the catalog for policy, and tell a student that the catalog edition covering 2026-2027 was not yet published at verification.
2. **Fall 2026 final examination week label.** The undergraduate calendar lists a final examination week labeled "Mon–Fri" against the dates Dec 9–11, 2026, which fall Wednesday–Friday, and separately lists "Final Examinations, Wed–Fri, Dec 9–11." The day-of-week label and the dates do not agree. Treat Dec 9–11, 2026 as the published exam dates and flag the label.
3. **Spring 2027 start date, undergraduate vs graduate.** Undergraduate: Thu, Jan 28, 2027. Graduate: Mon, Jan 25, 2027.
4. **Spring Break, undergraduate vs graduate.** Undergraduate: Mar 22–26, 2027. Graduate: Mar 15–19, 2027.
5. **Fall Break, undergraduate vs graduate.** Undergraduate: Oct 5–9, 2026. Graduate: Oct 6–9, 2026.
6. **ISP end date, undergraduate vs graduate.** Undergraduate January Interterm ends Jan 27, 2027. The graduate calendar gives ISP as Jan 4–22, 2027.
7. **Spring 2027 final examinations, undergraduate vs graduate.** Undergraduate lists reading days May 17–18 and examinations May 18–21, so May 18 appears as both. Graduate lists examinations May 19–21.
8. **Duplicated Spring 2027 evaluation deadlines.** "Course Designation and Evaluation Deadline" appears at both May 17 and May 25, 2027; "Contract Certification and Evaluation Deadline" appears at both May 18 and Jun 1, 2027. The calendar does not say which applies to which population. Do not pick one.
9. **PAOC and thesis prospectus milestones vs the catalog.** The calendar labels the Nov 2, 2026 and Apr 19, 2027 deadlines by **semester number** (5th semester PAOC, 6th semester thesis prospectus). The catalog states the provisional AOC is due "by the middle of the fourth attempted academic contract" and the thesis prospectus/AOC form by "5:00 p.m. on the first Friday of Module II" during the sixth contract — which for Fall 2026 would be Oct 16, 2026, not Nov 2, 2026. See `resources/students/degree-planning.md`. This conflict is unresolved in public sources.
10. **Leave-of-absence and off-campus-study declaration dates vs the catalog.** The calendar gives a combined "OCS & LOA Declaration Deadline" of Nov 2, 2026 and Apr 1, 2027. The catalog gives LOA deadlines of December 1 (spring) and May 1 (fall), and off-campus-study intent deadlines of November 1 (spring) and April 1 (fall). See `resources/students/isp-and-experiential-learning.md`.
11. **Juneteenth observance.** The undergraduate calendar shows Juneteenth observed Fri, Jun 18, 2027; the graduate calendar lists Juneteenth on Sat, Jun 19, 2027. June 19, 2027 falls on a Saturday, so these are plausibly the holiday and its observance, but the calendars do not say so.

12. **Residency reclassification deadline label.** The undergraduate calendar publishes this deadline as "Wed, Jun 26" for Fall 2026. June 26, 2026 is a Friday; the preceding Wednesday is June 24. The label and the date do not agree and this resource does not choose between them.

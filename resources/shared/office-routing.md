# Office Routing: Who Owns What at New College

Scope: which New College office owns a given task, so any of the three audiences can
be sent to the right place the first time. This file is deliberately built around
**office landing pages**, which are stable; phone numbers and email aliases are
included because routing needs them, but they are dated and reviewable. Emergencies
and sensitive topics have their own file: `resources/shared/sensitive-referrals.md`.

**Verified through: 2026-08-31**

**Audience:** shared — students, faculty, and outside enquirers.
**Volatility:** landing pages are stable; phone numbers, email aliases, room numbers,
and office hours are annual-volatility facts verified on 2026-08-31 and should be
re-checked at least yearly.

> **How to use this file.** Give the office name and its landing page. Add the phone
> or email alias only when the person needs to make contact now. Prefer an office
> alias (`registrar@ncf.edu`) over an individual's address — individuals change and
> the alias survives. Never state office hours or a phone number without saying when
> they were verified.

## Escalation ownership

When someone has already tried the owning office and is dissatisfied, the public
Report a Problem page describes the ladder. In order:

1. **Informal first.** The page states that resolving concerns informally at the
   grass-roots level is more likely to produce a timely resolution, and encourages a
   face-to-face meeting with the faculty member, supervisor, or other person involved.
2. **Ombudsperson** — `ombuds@ncf.edu`, for informal, impartial, confidential help.
3. **Formal written student complaint** — for a student who believes a published policy
   was violated or applied unfairly, or whose informal attempts failed. The submission
   form requires myNCF single sign-on. The page states NCF strives to respond within 30
   days and resolve within 90 days.
4. **Topic-specific offices** — Title IX for sex discrimination; Internal Audit and
   Compliance for waste, fraud, or financial mismanagement; the IRB for research
   non-compliance; Campus Police for safety and security.
5. **External bodies**, named on the page: the Florida Board of Governors State
   University System (800-245-0466), the Board of Governors Office of the Inspector
   General, SACSCOC as the institutional accreditor, FL-SARA for out-of-state distance
   education students, the Florida Commission on Ethics (850-488-7864), the Florida
   Commission on Human Relations (850-488-7082), the CFO Get Lean hotline
   (1-800-438-5326), the Florida Whistleblower's Hotline, and the U.S. Department of
   Education Office for Civil Rights (800-421-3481) and Office of Inspector General.

Describe the ladder; do not predict where a specific complaint will land or how it will
be decided.

## Known routing quirks

- **`/cwc/` redirects** to `/life-at-new/health-wellness-services/`. The Campus Police
  emergency page still labels two different links "Counseling and Wellness Services" and
  "Crisis Intervention and Victim Advocate," and both land on the general wellness hub
  rather than a victim-advocate page. Cite the hub and name the service you mean.
- **`/life-at-new/safety-conduct/title-ix/` redirects** to the Title IX and Civil
  Rights Compliance department page. Both URLs work; prefer the department page as
  canonical.
- **The START Center serves two functions** — Student Accounts and Parking Services
  share a phone number and room. Say which one you mean.
- **`/departments/academic-learning-compacts/` and
  `/departments/provosts-office/academic-learning-compacts/`** both resolve to the same
  page.
- **Individuals move.** Several office pages list staff by name; at least one card on
  the Provost's Office page links to a different person's directory entry. Use the
  directory search rather than a name copied from a department page.

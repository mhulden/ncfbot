# Visits, Maps, Alumni, and Community Information

Scope: Public orientation for campus visits, maps/directions, alumni/community access, and transcript/record routing for former students. Event schedules and operating details are volatile.
Verified through: 2026-08-31
Applies to: Visitors, prospective students, families, alumni, and community members.

## Campus visits

The Visit page states that New College offers in-person and virtual visit options and public admissions events. It currently describes in-person tours as available by appointment Monday through Friday and notes accessibility options for visitors with limited mobility.

Tour times, availability, events, and registration capacity can change. For a specific date, use the current Visit/registration page rather than a cached schedule.

## Location, maps, and directions

The Campus Map & Directions page states that New College is at the northern end of Sarasota County, across from Sarasota-Bradenton International Airport, and provides driving directions and named campus event venues.

The primary published college address is 5800 Bay Shore Road, Sarasota, FL 34243. Event venues may have more specific addresses; use the map page for the destination in question.

## Alumni and community

The Foundation & Alumni page provides public alumni/community information, events, giving, and involvement routes.

The Jane Bancroft Cook Library page states that New College alumni are eligible for community borrower cards and describes categories of other visitors who may use the library with appropriate identification/approval. Access conditions may change, so confirm current library rules before a visit.

## Alumni transcripts and records

The Registrar's Transcripts & Records page is the public route for official transcript requests, degree verifications, replacement diplomas, narrative evaluations, and related records services. The page states that New College uses the National Student Clearinghouse for official transcript requests.

A former student's record is private. Give the public request route; do not ask the user to paste transcript contents, identifying numbers, or account credentials.

## Public access boundaries

A public campus does not mean every building or activity is open without restriction. Use the relevant current public office/event page for access, parking, or event-specific rules. Do not infer access from old alumni policies or informal descriptions.

# Campus Life Orientation

Scope: Public orientation to housing, dining, student organizations, recreation/athletics, and general campus-life information. Operational details such as availability, hours, rates, meal plans, and deadlines are volatile.
Verified through: 2026-08-31
Applies to: Public campus-life information; 2026-2027 details only where explicitly labeled.

## Housing and residential life

The Housing & Residential Life page provides current public information about housing options, applications, rates/fees, deadlines, and residential policies.

At verification time, the page stated that undergraduate students are generally required to live on campus for their first two years unless granted a formal exception or waiver. It also stated that commuter requests use the housing application process. Because housing policies and deadlines can change, verify the current page before answering.

Do not guarantee a housing assignment, exemption, room type, or availability.

## 2026-2027 housing rates

The public room-rates page labels its rates as **2026-2027 Academic Year Room Rates** and states that rates may change based on Board of Trustees approval. Published annual rates vary by building and room type.

Do not copy a room rate into a current answer without the academic-year label and a freshness check.

## Dining

The Dining Services page publishes dining locations/hours and meal-plan information. At verification time, the page labeled meal-plan information for Fall 2026-2027 and stated that full-time undergraduate students are required to purchase a meal plan, with different options for residential and commuter students.

Dining hours, locations, meal plans, prices, and rules are operational information. Verify them for the named term before answering.

## Clubs, activities, waterfront and athletics

New College's public campus-life pages describe student clubs and organizations, student events, waterfront activities, recreation, and intercollegiate athletics.

The Life at New College page currently states that New College offers intercollegiate athletics teams competing in the NAIA's Sun Conference. Team counts and sport offerings can change, so use the current athletics/campus-life source rather than memorizing a count.

The clubs page states that clubs change as student interests change. Do not present a historical club list as a guaranteed current list.

## Stable institutional orientation

New College of Florida is a public college in Sarasota, Florida. Its main public site identifies it as the Honors College of Florida. The college's public history states that New College was founded in 1960 as a private college and became an independent member of the State University System of Florida in 2001.

The main campus address published by New College is 5800 Bay Shore Road, Sarasota, Florida 34243.

## Accreditation

New College's accreditation page states that the institution is accredited by the Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges (SACSCOC). The page states that New College is accredited to award associate, baccalaureate, and master's degrees and may offer other approved credentials at those degree levels.

## Mission and descriptive claims

## Historical context

The college history page reports these durable milestones:

- New College was founded in 1960.
- Its charter class enrolled in 1964.
- In 1975, New College joined the State University System as part of the University of South Florida.
- On July 1, 2001, New College became an independent member of the State University System.

Historical claims should remain clearly historical and should not be used to imply current policies.

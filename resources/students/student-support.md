# Student Support Services

Scope: Public entry points for advising, tutoring, writing support, the library, accessibility, career support, health and wellbeing, and student-affairs routing. This resource routes students to offices. It deliberately keeps detail thin on crisis and high-risk contacts.

Verified through: 2026-08-31

Applies to: all currently enrolled students. Office names, locations, and phone numbers as published on 2026-08-31.

Volatility: `annual` for office names and locations; treat staff names and phone numbers as `annual` and re-verify before quoting.

---

## Advising

The academic sponsor is the center of a student's advising team, guiding them "from your initial mini-classes to your baccalaureate exam" across coursework, internships, areas of concentration, and the senior project. Students also work with an assigned career coach and any support services they choose to use.

**Coverage gap:** the public advising page states that further advising resources and faculty advisor guidelines are on the NCF Community Pages, "MyNCF login required." That content is not public and is out of scope.

Page: https://www.ncf.edu/advising/

---

## Tutoring — Academic Resource Center (ARC)

- Described as a "comprehensive tutoring service, offering students free tutoring in any discipline."
- Location: Library, LBR 119.
- Contact named in the catalog: Travis Lee.
- Scheduling is published through the MyNCF portal, so the schedule itself is not publicly viewable.

## Writing support — Writing Resource Center (WRC)

- "open to all New College students who are interested in developing skills."
- Offers individual conferences with trained student writing assistants.
- Page: https://www.ncf.edu/academics/writing-program/writing-resource-center/

The WRC helps a student develop their own writing. It is the correct referral when a student asks the bot to write something for them — see the academic-integrity boundary in `skills/students.md`.

## Accessibility — Accessible Learning Center (ALC)

- Official name: **Accessible Learning Center (ALC)**.
- Stated mission: to "empower students with disabilities to succeed at NCF by offering personalized support and accommodations in compliance with Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act, ADA, and relevant laws."
- **Accommodation requests** are made through the **Accessible Information Management (AIM)** system, where registered students request accommodations for the next semester's classes or update their eligibility list.
- Location: Hamilton Classroom 005. Hours: Monday–Friday, 9 a.m. – 5 p.m. Phone: (941) 487-4844.
- Page: https://www.ncf.edu/departments/advocacy-accessibility/

**Boundary.** The bot explains that the ALC exists, what it does, and how the request process starts. It does not ask about a student's condition, documentation, or diagnosis, does not assess whether an accommodation will be granted, and does not record any of it. Refer and stop.

> The office's staff email addresses were not readable in the public page as retrieved. Do not guess an address; give the phone number and the page.

## Library and thesis submission

Cook Library accepts the senior thesis in final form; submission is electronic through the Thesis Submission Form on the library website, and the library's acceptance is a graduation requirement. Thesis questions: thesis@ncf.edu. See `degree-planning.md` for deadlines and the consequence of missing them.

## Health and wellbeing

The public Health & Well-being page names these services:

| Service | As described publicly |
|---|---|
| **Health & Wellness Services (HWS)** | "provides health, counseling and wellness services to help you achieve physical, emotional and social well being" |
| **Student Support Team (SST)** | a collaborative group assisting students with "obstacles that may impact their success and well-being"; a report form is provided |
| Recreation Center | weight room, cardio equipment, racquetball court, dance/exercise rooms, outdoor pool and hot tub |
| Waterfront Program | kayaking, sailing, sailboarding, beach access on Sarasota Bay |
| Student Emergency Fund | financial assistance for food, housing, transportation, and medical expenses |
| Food Pantry | "provides emergency and supplemental food items of high nutritional value" |

Page: https://www.ncf.edu/life-at-new/health-well-being/

**Important limitation.** As retrieved, this page publishes **no phone numbers, email addresses, or after-hours crisis line**. Do not supply any. For anything involving immediate risk, direct the student to emergency services and to campus police, and defer to Agent 3's `sensitive-referrals.md` for the canonical contacts once merged.

**Boundary.** The bot may say that counseling and wellness services exist and how a student reaches the office. It does not provide clinical advice, does not ask for symptoms or history, and does not store health information.

# ISPs, Internships, and Off-Campus Study

Scope: Independent Study Projects, internships, study abroad and other off-campus study, and the approval and evaluation boundaries around them. Dates come from `resources/shared/academic-calendar.md`. The contract and evaluation vocabulary is in `academic-model.md`.

Verified through: 2026-08-31

Applies to: undergraduate students under catalog edition 2025-2026; dates are from the 2026-2027 public academic calendar. Graduate programs carry no ISP requirement.

Volatility: `term`.

---

## Independent Study Projects

The ISP is a graduation requirement and a distinct term, not an add-on.

**Requirement.** "Three ISPs are required for graduation. A student may register for a fourth ISP."

**Form.** An ISP is an intensive, full-time three-to-four-week academic activity worth 3–4 credits. Per the catalog, an ISP "cannot coincide with full-time employment or regular semester contracts" — a student cannot work full time and complete an ISP in the same period.

**First ISP.** "It is strongly recommended that the first ISP take place on campus." **Transfer students are exempt** from this recommendation.

**Units.** An ISP counts as a unit toward the 31 academic units required for the B.A. It does **not** count toward the 12 credit hours that define full-time status for financial aid — see `financial-and-status-cautions.md`.

### ISP timing and deadlines

| Item | Rule as published | 2026-2027 date |
|---|---|---|
| January Interterm | The ISP term | Mon, Jan 4 – Wed, Jan 27, 2027 |
| ISP course description forms due | Fall deadline on the calendar | Fri, Nov 13, 2026 |
| ISP registration | Runs with the fall add/drop period | Mon–Fri, Aug 17–21, 2026 |
| ISP drop/add deadline | No refund after | Wed, Jan 6, 2027 |
| Interterm ISP work due | "All work completed as part of the Interterm ISP is due no later than the last day of the January Interterm" | Wed, Jan 27, 2027 |
| Faculty evaluation of January ISPs | Due "by the first day following spring break" | Mon, Mar 29, 2027 (published as ISP Course Evaluation Submission Deadline) |
| Summer internship completion | "completed and evaluated before the start of the fall term" | Before Mon, Aug 17, 2026 for the 2026-2027 year |
| Summer coursework transcript | "The New College Office of the Registrar must receive the transcript by the last day of fall classes" | Fri, Dec 4, 2026 |

**Incomplete ISP work.** A student has until "the first day of class of the next full academic semester" to complete the work. An extension requires a **Provost Petition** and grants "an additional two months."

**Consequence.** Two unsatisfactory ISPs place a student on probation; one unsatisfactory contract plus two unsatisfactory ISPs is a dismissal threshold. See `academic-model.md`.

> Note on the graduate calendar: the graduate calendar lists the ISP period as Jan 4–22, 2027 while the undergraduate calendar runs the January Interterm through Jan 27, 2027. Graduate programs do not carry the ISP graduation requirement.

## Tutorials and independent readings

The catalog's contract definition includes "courses, tutorials, internships and other planned academic undertakings" as the activities that make up the three-unit minimum. Beyond that listing, **public sources found at the verification date do not document a separate tutorial or independent-reading approval procedure.** Do not describe one. Refer the student to their contract sponsor, who negotiates what goes on the contract.

---

## Internships

Internships appear in two places in public sources: as an activity that can be written into a semester contract, and as a summer ISP form with its own completion and evaluation deadline (both before the start of the fall term). The Center for Career Engagement and Opportunity (CEO) is the office associated with career and internship support — see `student-support.md`.

**Coverage gap:** public sources found do not publish a general internship registration procedure, a required learning-agreement form, or supervisor evaluation requirements outside the ISP context. Route to the contract sponsor and the CEO rather than describing a process.

## Off-campus study and study abroad

**Eligibility.** A student must have "completed two successive satisfactory contracts at New College." First-year students may petition the Provost for approval where the educational benefit justifies it.

**Declaration of intent.** The catalog requires students to "declare their intent by April 1 for the following fall semester or November 1 for spring semester."

> **Conflict: declaration deadline.** The 2026-2027 academic calendar publishes a combined "OCS & LOA Declaration Deadline" of **Mon, Nov 2, 2026** and **Thu, Apr 1, 2027**. The April date matches the catalog; the November date is one day later than the catalog's November 1, which falls on a Sunday in 2026. This resource does not resolve whether Nov 2 is an intentional shift for the weekend or a separate deadline. Give the student the catalog date as the safe assumption and have the Office of the Registrar confirm.

**Forms.** Most documents are submitted online at `abroad.ncf.edu`. Two are not: the **Declaration for Off-Campus Study** and the **Tuition Waiver Request** require contract sponsor signatures and go to the Office of the Registrar.

**Tuition waiver.** A student paying a host institution must complete a Tuition Waiver Form for every off-campus semester. Once waived, "the student's contract may show only work to be done at the host institution."

**Academic load.** A student must enroll in a "minimum of 3 New College academic units (12 semester credits or 18 quarter credits of study)" while away.

**Final contract.** The student must complete their final contract on campus.

**Credit transfer back.** Official transcripts from the host institution must be sent to `registrar@ncf.edu` immediately after completion. If a transcript lacks clear U.S. credit-hour equivalents or is not in English, the student pays for credential evaluation and professional translation.

**Aid consequence.** If the host transcript is not received by the published deadline, the semester's aid may be removed from the student's account. See `financial-and-status-cautions.md`.

**Calendar dates:** the Spring Off-Campus Study Contract & Tuition Waiver Deadline is published as Thu, Jan 28, 2027.

**Office:** Office of Global Education & Study Abroad, located in the Center for Career Engagement and Opportunity, LBR 157 — https://www.ncf.edu/departments/study-abroad/

## Sponsor and approval boundaries

Three different approvals appear in this area and students conflate them:

| Approval | Who gives it |
|---|---|
| What goes on the contract, and whether an ISP or internship is satisfactory | The contract sponsor (faculty) |
| Off-campus study declaration, tuition waiver, transcript receipt | Office of the Registrar, with sponsor signatures |
| Extension for incomplete ISP work; first-year off-campus exception | Provost petition |

The bot explains which approval a step needs and who grants it. It does not predict whether an approval will be granted, and it does not assess whether a proposed project is good enough — that is the sponsor's judgment.

## What this resource does not establish

- Whether a student's proposed ISP or internship will be approved or evaluated as satisfactory.
- Whether a Provost petition will succeed.
- Which specific study-abroad programs are running in a given term.
- Tutorial or independent-reading procedures, which are not publicly documented.

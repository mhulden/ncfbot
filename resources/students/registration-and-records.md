# Registration and Student Records

Scope: How undergraduate students register, submit and change a contract, drop or withdraw from courses, take a leave of absence, withdraw from the College, seek readmission, and obtain records. Dates come from `resources/shared/academic-calendar.md`. Aid and status consequences are in `financial-and-status-cautions.md`. Course availability is out of scope here — see the course resources owned by Agent 6.

Verified through: 2026-08-31

Applies to: undergraduate students under catalog edition 2025-2026; dates are from the 2026-2027 public academic calendar.

Volatility: `term`.

## Public versus authenticated steps

This distinction matters for every question in this resource.

| Step | Public? |
|---|---|
| Browsing the class schedule | **Public.** The Registrar publishes a public class-search tool at `https://banapps02.ncf.edu/StudentRegistrationSsb/ssb/term/termSelection?mode=search` — select a term, then search. |
| Historical course data | **Public.** The Registrar links a Power BI database covering five academic years back to Fall 2022. |
| Syllabi and textbooks | **Public** via the SimpleSyllabus portal for Fall 2026 forward. |
| Registering for courses | **Authenticated.** Banner 9 Student Self Service, reached through the Class Schedule tile in MyNCF. |
| Submitting or renegotiating a contract | **Authenticated / office process.** |
| Most Registrar forms | **Authenticated.** `intranet.ncf.edu/registrar-forms/` requires MyNCF login. |
| Withdrawal Request form, Readmission Application | Online forms hosted on third-party systems linked from the Registrar page. |

**Never claim to know what is behind MyNCF, Banner, or the intranet.** Say the step requires login, name the office, and stop.

## Contract submission

The contract is the enrollment document; registering for courses is not sufficient by itself.

- Due "on the first Friday of the semester" — Fri, Aug 21, 2026 for Fall 2026.
- Late submission accepted with a fee "until close of business on the second Friday of the semester" — Fri, Aug 28, 2026.
- A student who has not submitted by "5:00 p.m. on the second Friday of the semester will have their enrollment canceled, unless the student successfully petitions the Provost."

**Contract renegotiation deadlines (2026-2027):** Fri, Nov 13, 2026 and Fri, Apr 23, 2027. The catalog separately gives "Friday of the second week of the semester" as the deadline for adding or dropping courses within the contract, so a student asking "can I still change my contract?" needs to be asked *what kind of change* and *which deadline*.

**Contract certification and evaluation deadlines (2026-2027):** Mon, Dec 21, 2026 for Fall 2026; Tue, May 18, 2027 for Spring 2027 (with a second date of Tue, Jun 1, 2027 also published — see discrepancy 8 in the academic calendar resource).

## Dropping a course

- **During add/drop:** drop online through Student Self Service "until the end of the add/drop period for the term."
- **After add/drop:** submit a drop request through the online Add/Drop form.
- **Notation:** before the final drop deadline, a dropped course receives an automatic "withdrawn" designation. After the final drop deadline, failing to submit a completed drop form results in an **unsatisfactory designation** — the course is not simply removed.
- The catalog does not print drop dates; it says to "Refer to the academic calendar for drop deadlines."

**Published drop and withdrawal dates, Fall 2026:** Module I last day to withdraw Wed, Sep 23, 2026; last day to withdraw or drop a class (no refund) Tue, Nov 10, 2026; Module II last day to withdraw Mon, Nov 23, 2026.
**Spring 2027:** Module I last day to withdraw Wed, Feb 24, 2027; last day to withdraw or drop a spring class Fri, Apr 16, 2027.

Dropping below three units has aid and status consequences — see `financial-and-status-cautions.md` before answering.

## Withdrawing from the College

A full withdrawal is different from dropping a course. A student "wishing to drop all courses and withdraw from the institution for the term must initiate a formal withdrawal through the online Withdrawal Request form" submitted to the Office of the Registrar.

- **Last date of attendance** is "the earliest date recorded on your Withdrawal Request form received by the Office of the Registrar" or a documented academically-related activity, whichever comes first.
- The catalog's departure checklist: notify the faculty advisor, meet with Financial Aid, check with Housing, inform professors.
- **ISP tuition** is refundable only if the student withdraws "by the end of the add/drop period."
- **Ceasing to attend without withdrawing carries the same financial aid consequences as a formal withdrawal.** State this whenever a student describes stopping attendance.
- Returning after a withdrawal requires applying for **readmission**, not simply registering.

**Refund dates published for 2026-2027:** last day for 25% tuition/fees refund for full withdrawal — Fri, Sep 11, 2026 (Fall) and Wed, Feb 24, 2027 (Spring).

> **Gap between sources.** The catalog's tuition and fee assessment policy describes a 100% refund for withdrawal "prior to, or during, the second week of classes" and does not publish a graduated schedule, while the academic calendar publishes 25% refund dates. The two do not together form a complete refund table. Do not construct one. Send the student to the Office of the Registrar and Student Accounts.

## Leave of absence

| Item | As published in the catalog |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | "The student must complete one full academic year at New College and be in good academic standing"; no holds; good financial standing |
| Request | Leave of Absence form to the Office of the Registrar, "signed by the student's faculty advisor"; if the advisor declines, appeal to the Dean of Studies |
| Deadlines | December 1 for a spring LOA; May 1 for a fall LOA |
| Timing | LOAs may be requested only between semesters |
| Maximum | "A Leave of Absence may be granted for up to two consecutive terms" |
| On return | The student "will be placed on academic probation" ordinarily; the cumulative record may be reviewed by the Student Academic Status Committee; a student may petition to waive certain unsatisfactory designations with faculty advisor approval |
| Before returning | Clear balances and conduct matters; consult financial aid about eligibility; returning late triggers a readmission review |

> **Conflict: LOA deadline.** The catalog gives December 1 and May 1. The 2026-2027 academic calendar publishes a combined "OCS & LOA Declaration Deadline" of Mon, Nov 2, 2026 and Thu, Apr 1, 2027. These do not agree. Give the student both dates, note that the earlier one is the safer assumption, and route to the Office of the Registrar for the operative deadline.

The automatic probation on return is a consequence students rarely anticipate. State it.

## Readmission

- Published deadlines to request readmission, 2026-2027: **Mon, Nov 2, 2026** (for spring) and **Thu, Apr 1, 2027** (for fall).
- The Registrar publishes a Readmission Application on an online forms system and a public "Readmission Procedures" PDF.
- A student readmitted after a prior dismissal faces a stricter dismissal threshold — one unsatisfactory contract. See `academic-model.md`.

## Records

**Official transcripts.** The Registrar's records page names the **National Student Clearinghouse** as the vendor, at `https://tsorder.studentclearinghouse.org/school/select`, with a **$10.00 fee per transcript** and processing "usually ... within three (3) business days." Rush and express options cost extra.

> **Conflict: transcript vendor and fee.** The catalog's transcript page states that official transcripts are requested through **GetMyTranscript.com**, that "Transcripts are generally processed within 3-7 business days," and that "There is a fee" without naming an amount. The Registrar's own records page names a different vendor, a $10.00 fee, and a three-business-day turnaround. Both are official public NCF pages and they disagree. Prefer the Registrar's records page as the office responsible for the transaction, tell the user the catalog says something different, and have them confirm with the Office of the Registrar before paying.

**Other record services** listed by the Registrar:

- **Degree verification** — National Student Clearinghouse (`https://nscverifications.org/welcome-to-verification-services/`).
- **Enrollment verification** — no fee; submit the Request for Verification of Enrollment form to the Registrar.
- **Certification of graduation** — form provided by the Registrar.
- **Replacement diploma** — Diploma Replacement Request Form; "approximately two weeks to process."
- **Narrative evaluation copies** — Narrative Evaluation Request Form. This is the New College-specific one; a student who needs their evaluations for a graduate application should be pointed here, not to the transcript order.
- **Change of address** — public PDF form.

**Coverage gaps:** neither page describes unofficial transcripts, name changes, FERPA release procedures, or which holds block a transcript. Do not fill these in.
